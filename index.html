The Transfer Test

A model that works at one site is moved to another. Find out what breaks, fix what you can afford, and decide whether to use it.

Built by Cynthia McGinnis. All data are simulated.

What do you recommend?

What this exercise simplifies. The records are generated by the page. They are not real people, vehicles, or students. The exercise assumes that acting on a flag prevents the outcome, and that a human reviewer always gets a close call right. Real deployments are messier on both counts.
